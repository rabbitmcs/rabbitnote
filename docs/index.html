RabbitNote

A private notebook for Linux. One ruled paper page per day, with a calendar, #tags and search.

Yours alone

Notes and images are encrypted on your computer with AES-256. Only your passphrase opens them, and a forgotten passphrase can't be recovered.

On all your computers

Sign in with Google to sync through your own Drive. RabbitNote only touches its own files, and catches up after being offline.

Make it your own

Add images, tables and drawings, and change any font or color.
